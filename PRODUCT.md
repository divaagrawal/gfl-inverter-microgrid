# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS dashboard plus Python simulation so the project remains portable and reproducible.

## Users

Recruiters and early-career power-system and power-electronics engineers reviewing a technical portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate how grid-following and grid-forming battery inverters behave differently during an islanding disturbance, load step, and black start.

## Positioning

A transparent synthetic dynamic benchmark that visualizes control behavior and limits rather than claiming utility-grade or hardware-in-the-loop validation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

All feeder, load, inverter, and event parameters are synthetic. Results are educational, not a grid-code compliance study or a real microgrid design.

## Evidence on Hand

Saved simulation results, deterministic Python code, and automated tests. No physical measurements or vendor validation data.

## Product Principles

- Show the disturbance and response before describing the controls.
- Preserve technical traceability and label every synthetic input.
- Make critical operating limits visually unmistakable.
